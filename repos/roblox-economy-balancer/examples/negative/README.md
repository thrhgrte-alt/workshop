# Negative examples (avoid these)

Generated by `scripts/make_examples.py`. **Synthetic toy economies, each with one deliberate defect.** Every one must be flagged by `check_economy`; the balanced toy must not be.

| Economy | Library id | Defect | Findings |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wall.yaml` (`wall.png`) | `econ-wall` | `drill_4` costs 32000 against 80 income per minute: 400 active minutes for regular players, 20x the balanced price | `wall`, `cost_cliff`, `gap_cliff`, `dead_option`, `slow_for_archetype` |
| `runaway.yaml` (`runaway.png`) | `econ-runaway` | income 100 per minute against costs 100, 300, 900: everything is bought within minutes and the currency floods | `under_band`, `content_exhausted`, `runaway_inflation` |
| `dominant.yaml` (`dominant.png`) | `econ-dominant` | at tiers 2 and 3 option A costs the same as B and gives twice the income | `dominant_option`, `dominated_option`, `dominant_strategy` |

`projects/demo_tycoon/main/economy.yaml` registers the runaway toy as a synthetic place. `examples/playtest/tier4_felt_slow.json` is a synthetic playtest note in the shape `suggest_band_adjustments` reads.
