# Style: readable, cohesive concept art for stylised Roblox worlds (starter template)

Human-readable companion to `style.yaml` (the machine-readable source). **A starting point, not your art direction.** Replace it with what your game needs.

## Traits
- **Value structure:** three clear value groups; the focal point separates from its surround by value first, colour second.
- **Palette:** a dominant family, one secondary, one accent; shadows are tinted, not black.
- **Silhouette:** environments have a recognisable skyline; props read in solid black at 64 px.
- **Shape language:** chunky, simplified forms for stylised low-poly or lightly textured builds.
- **Detail:** concentrated near the focal point; large areas stay calm.
- **Scale:** human-scale cues (doors, steps, railings) in environments.

## Hard rules
Concept art only (never presented as a mesh, graph or scene); every run recorded; several directions that differ on >= 3 axes; only licensed or owned references/training images; AI outputs enter the
library only after a person accepts them.

## Avoid
Text or watermarks, near-duplicate "variations", props with scenery or cropped, environments with no value separation, copies of a reference.

## Measured vs judged
Value range, edge density, palette adherence, silhouette stats, focal contrast and novelty are **measured**. Brief adherence, usability as a reference, and the human half of style cohesion,
readability and prop silhouette are **judged by a person** and stay unscored until then. The ranges are heuristics: tune them on concepts you consider good.
