# Licensing of assets

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the **code, schemas and documentation** in this repository.

It does **not** grant any rights to assets you add. Your reference library is yours:

- Only ingest assets you made, or have permission to use as references. Record the owner in
  `license.owner` (`user`, `user-approved`, `third-party`). Entries with `unknown` owner stay
  `candidate` and are never returned by default searches.
- The files under `examples/` are **synthetic placeholders** generated by scripts in this repo
  so tests and demos run. They are released under the same MIT license and are not real
  production assets.
- Keep private or third-party assets outside Git (point `CONCEPTAI_ASSET_ROOT` at them). This
  repository never uploads assets anywhere. If you use a hosted image or embedding service,
  check that service's terms for what happens to the data you send it.
- Fine-tuning a model on someone else's work may be restricted by their license. Check
  before building a training set.

## Concept-art specifics

- **Generated images.** Who owns an image a model generates, and what you may do with it, depends on the generator's terms and on your jurisdiction. This repository
  records the declared model, adapter, prompt, seed and reference ids for every generation so you can answer that question later; it cannot answer it for you.
- **Reference images.** `extract_reference_traits` reads only pixels (palette, value range, edge density). Use references you made or are licensed to use. Copying a
  reference is not inspiration: the novelty check exists to catch near-copies of your *own* library, not of other people's work.
- **AI outputs in the library.** An accepted generated image enters the library flagged `ai_generated` and `concept_only`, with `provenance.origin = ai-generated`. It never
  looks human-made, and `lora_validate_dataset` caps how many such images a training set may contain.
- **LoRA / PEFT.** `dataset.yaml` must state a licence and an owner for every image. The default policy allows `own-work`, `licensed-for-training`, `cc0`, `cc-by-4.0`
  (attribution required) and refuses everything else, including "unknown". `style/lora_policy.yaml` is a policy file you control; it is not legal advice, and the
  validator cannot tell whether a licence statement is true. Check the base model's licence too: it may restrict fine-tuning or sharing derivatives.
- **Roblox.** Concept images are references for people and tools. Uploading images or meshes to Roblox has its own rules and moderation; nothing here uploads anything.
