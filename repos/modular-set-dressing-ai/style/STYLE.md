# Style: lived-in, readable set dressing (starter template)

Human-readable companion to `style.yaml` (the machine-readable source). **A starting point, not your art direction.** Replace it with what your game needs.

## Traits
- **Hierarchy:** one hero piece, a few large supports, many small accents.
- **Grouping:** props cluster around furniture (chairs at tables, items on surfaces), they do not scatter evenly.
- **Repetition:** no module above 40% of the scene; neighbouring props differ.
- **Negative space:** one open rectangle of at least ~12% of the region.
- **Colour:** a dominant family with one or two accents.
- **Scale:** stay inside each module's stated scale range.

## Hard rules
Only approved modules; locked pieces untouched; functional paths and sightlines clear; plans reviewed and reversible.

## Avoid
One prop copied across the region, furniture clipping through furniture, props blocking doors/paths/sightlines, evenly spaced prop grids.

## Measured vs judged
Coverage, repetition, colour spread, free space, focal satisfaction, hierarchy and all clearance checks are **measured**. `reads_as_lived_in`, `matches_style` and
`supports_gameplay` are **judged by a person** and stay unscored until then. Ranges are heuristics: tune them against scenes you consider good.
